# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

Menreiki does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Ever.

- **No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.** The application makes
  no network connections of its own.
- **All processing is local.** Document analysis, OCR, transformation, and
  auditing run entirely on your machine. Your documents and everything
  derived from them (page images, OCR text, review decisions) stay in
  project folders on your device, under your control.
- **Optional local AI assistance** connects only to an OpenAI-compatible
  endpoint on `localhost` that you configure yourself. Connections to any
  non-localhost address are rejected by construction. No document content
  leaves your machine through this feature.
- **No accounts.** The application requires no sign-in and has no server
  side.

This applies identically to every distribution channel (GitHub Releases,
winget, Microsoft Store).

Changes to this policy are visible in the
[repository history](https://github.com/ShortArrow/Menreiki/commits/main/docs/PRIVACY.md).
